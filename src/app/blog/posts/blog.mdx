## About

It all began as a small spark — a desire to have a **digital space that truly reflected me**.  
I had previously built a portfolio on **WordPress**, which at the time felt like a solid start. But as I grew, I started feeling **limited by its constraints** — the lack of flexibility, the minimal customization, and the constant struggle to add modern elements without paying for premium upgrades.  

There were times I felt **stuck**, creatively and technically. I wanted a platform where I could **experiment freely**, learn, and build something that matched my evolving journey in cybersecurity and technology.

One random day, while scrolling through **LinkedIn**, I came across a post about **free portfolio hosting on Vercel**. It instantly caught my attention.  
That short post opened a whole new world to me — a world of developers sharing ideas, deploying creative projects, and showcasing their work with **complete freedom**.

Driven by curiosity, I began exploring frameworks and templates. That’s when I found **One UI**, created by **Selena Yu** — a simple yet beautifully designed portfolio structure that immediately clicked with me. It wasn’t just modern; it felt alive.

## My Contribution

As a **cybersecurity student**, I wanted my portfolio to represent not just my work, but also my personality — my curiosity, my passion for systems, and my love for building secure and interactive tools.  

So, I added my own touch:  
an **inbuilt terminal** within the website — a unique element that allows users to interact with my portfolio in a command-line style interface. It blended creativity and identity, making the portfolio not just a static page, but a **living reflection** of who I am.

## Reflection

What began as a simple search on LinkedIn became **a journey of rediscovery and creation**. I learned how much freedom and satisfaction comes from **building something from the ground up**, where every pixel and function has a purpose.  

This experience didn’t just give me a new website — it **motivated me to keep exploring, creating, and learning** without limits.  
Right now, as I’m writing this post, I’m still refining and improving it — one commit at a time — and I can’t help but feel proud of how far I’ve come.

This portfolio project became more than a digital resume; it became **a reflection of growth, creativity, and perseverance** — a reminder that every limitation can spark a new beginning.
