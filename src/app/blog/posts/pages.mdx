## About

**AGM 2024** was held on **9th April 2025** at the **IEEE Kerala Section, Kochi**, beginning at **10:00 AM**.  
It wasn’t just another meeting — it was a day that brought together the entire crew of the **IEEE EdSoc Kerala Chapter Execom**, a family bound by shared memories, hard work, and endless laughter.

The atmosphere was light-hearted and full of warmth. We chatted, played games, and laughed together — moments that reminded me how strong the bonds within the team had become over time. Even though it was the time of fasting for many, the joy of being together made the day effortlessly cheerful and energetic.

One of the most unforgettable parts of the day was being **recognized for my hard work** at EdSoc. Receiving an award from **Dr. K. Kesavasamy** felt both humbling and motivating — a small token that carried immense emotional weight. It was more than a prize; it was a symbol of gratitude, validation, and encouragement to keep pushing forward.

The day grew even more memorable with the incredible energy brought in by **Er. Sarath S**, our former Student Activities Chair, and **Er. Akshay A R**, the current Student Activities Chair. Their enthusiasm lit up the room, keeping everyone engaged and smiling through the games and interactive moments. 

I was also grateful to share the day with **Er. Diya**, who has always been like a sisterly figure to me. Her guidance, patience, and support through every challenge meant more than words could express. Standing beside her that day reminded me how much I’ve grown under the mentorship and kindness of those around me.

## Reflection

As the event drew to a close, a wave of nostalgia hit me.  
It dawned on me that this might be the last time I would see the whole team together — the people who shaped my journey, taught me lessons, and stood by me through every chapter.  

**AGM 2024** wasn’t just a ceremony of farewell; it was a bridge between the past and the future — a moment of moving out from the previous team and stepping into a new one, filled with fresh, energetic minds and exciting possibilities.  

I left that day with a full heart — thankful for the memories, the people, and the privilege of being part of something truly special. If I could, I would have frozen that moment just to relive it a little longer.
