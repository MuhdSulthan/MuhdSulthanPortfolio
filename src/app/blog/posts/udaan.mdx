## About

**UDAAN 2.0** was held on **4th January 2025** at the **Sree Narayana Gurukulam College of Engineering (SNGCE), Kadayiruppu**, from **9:30 AM to 4:00 PM**.  
Organized by the **IEEE Education Society Kerala Chapter** in collaboration with **IEEE SB SNGCE**, the event brought together **75 enthusiastic pre-university students** eager to explore the possibilities of engineering and technology.

The day began with a **warm welcome address**, followed by the **lamp lighting ceremony** led by **Sri K. N. Gopalakrishnan**, Treasurer of SNG Trust. The glow of the lamp symbolized the beginning of a day devoted to knowledge, inspiration, and youthful curiosity.

The **first session**, *“Gateway to Engineering: The Roadmap,”* by **Er. Vyasan J** — a Solopreneur, Angel Investor, and Freelance Data Engineering Technical Trainer — gave the students a clearer picture of the engineering journey ahead. His words sparked motivation and helped them envision a future built on curiosity and perseverance.

Next came *“The Future of Technology: An Introduction”* by **Hazna R. Mohammed**, the Membership Development Coordinator of the IEEE Education Society Kerala Chapter.  
Her session explored how innovation continues to reshape our world, inspiring students to think boldly and creatively about the technologies of tomorrow.

After the sessions, everyone embarked on a **guided campus tour** of **SNGCE**. It was wonderful watching the students’ excitement as they explored the labs, classrooms, and the overall academic environment — their curiosity was contagious.

The final session of the day, *“From Calculations to Conversations: Soft Skills for the Modern Engineer,”* led by **Dr. Maya Devi Menon**, was where everything became truly special for me.  
It was my **first time being in the center of attention**, surrounded by energetic students and guided by Dr. Maya’s incredible enthusiasm. The way she engaged not just the students but **me as well** turned the session into a moment of pure joy and excitement. Her interactive approach made everyone laugh, learn, and reflect — transforming the hall into a space filled with positivity and energy.  
That experience will always stay close to my heart, reminding me how impactful and uplifting a learning environment can be when filled with laughter and shared emotions.

## Reflection

As the event came to a close, the organizers expressed gratitude to everyone who contributed to the day’s success. The participants left with smiles, new knowledge, and a sense of inspiration that echoed through the campus.

**UDAAN 2.0** wasn’t just another event — it was a **day of memories**, laughter, and connection. It gave me the chance to interact with students, witness their dreams taking shape, and feel part of something bigger — a shared journey toward the future of engineering.

Walking away that evening, I couldn’t help but feel grateful — for the experience, the people, and the joy that came from being part of such a vibrant and meaningful day.
