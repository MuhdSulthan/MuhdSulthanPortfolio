## Overview

During my internship at Alibi Global Pvt. Ltd., I conducted comprehensive digital forensic analysis on multiple criminal case studies. This project involved evidence acquisition, data recovery, and forensic examination of various digital devices to support legal investigations.

## Key Features

- **Evidence Acquisition**: Forensically sound imaging and acquisition of digital evidence from smartphones, computers, and storage devices.
- **Data Recovery**: Advanced techniques to recover deleted files, communication records, and hidden data from damaged or encrypted devices.
- **Timeline Analysis**: Reconstruction of digital activities and events to establish chronological sequences for legal proceedings.
- **Report Generation**: Detailed forensic reports documenting findings, methodologies, and chain of custody for court presentation.

## Technologies Used

- **Magnet AXIOM**: Comprehensive digital investigation platform for evidence processing and analysis.
- **Cellebrite UFED 4PC**: Mobile forensics tool for smartphone data extraction and analysis.
- **True Imager**: Forensic imaging tool for creating bit-for-bit copies of digital storage media.
- **EnCase**: Digital forensics platform for evidence examination and case management.

## Case Studies Completed

### Case 1: Financial Fraud Investigation
- Recovered deleted transaction records from compromised banking application
- Analyzed communication patterns between suspects
- Identified money laundering schemes through digital evidence

### Case 2: Cyberbullying and Harassment
- Extracted social media communications and multimedia evidence
- Recovered deleted messages and images from mobile devices
- Established timeline of harassment activities

### Case 3: Corporate Data Theft
- Analyzed network logs and file access patterns
- Recovered stolen intellectual property from suspect devices
- Traced data exfiltration methods and timelines

## Challenges and Learnings

Working with encrypted devices and damaged storage media required advanced recovery techniques and patience. I learned the importance of maintaining strict chain of custody procedures and documenting every step of the forensic process. Understanding legal requirements and presenting technical findings in court-ready format was crucial for successful case outcomes.

## Outcome

Successfully analyzed 3 criminal cases with 100% evidence integrity maintained throughout the investigation process. Recovered critical evidence from 7+ devices that contributed to successful legal proceedings. The forensic reports provided clear, actionable intelligence that supported law enforcement investigations and judicial processes.
